# About

This site is run as a private initiative by Manu Eder and Sebastian Schubert. We have both been part of the Viennese CI community for years.
Our aim is to give people who are interested in CI as comprehensive an overview as possible of what is on offer in Vienna.
Our work is voluntary, we do not work on behalf of and are in no way dependent on the people and organisations presented on this website. At the same time, we endeavour to maintain a friendly relationship with all those involved. We are not active as organisers ourselves and do not derive any financial benefit from the operation of this website.
All information on dates and events is provided to us by the respective persons. We accept no liability for the accuracy of the information, but we always endeavour to keep all information up to date.

Content and page design: Sebastian Schubert and Manu Eder

If you are teaching contact improvisation in Vienna or organizing some other event with a clear focus on contact improvisation that is not listed here yet, please get in touch with [Sebastian](mailto:sebastianschubert@gmx.at) to get it listed.

Please send reports of technical problems with the site to [Manu](mailto:manulari@posteo.eu).

This site is served by github pages and generated from this source [github.com/...](https://github.com/manulari/contactimprovisationat/).

Fine print: We have no control over the content of the external pages linked to. Don't sue us if you don't like what they are saying.
